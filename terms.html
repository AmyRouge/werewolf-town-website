How to behave

Werewolf is a game about lying, so bluffing, accusing and betraying people inside the game is fine. Real harassment is not. Don't:

  • harass, threaten, hate on or bully other players, or use slurs;
  • share sexual content, personal information about anyone, or anything illegal;
  • cheat: no scripts, bots, exploits, or teaming up outside the game to leak secret roles or Wolf Pack / Graveyard chat;
  • spam chat, invites or reports, or deliberately ruin matches by leaving or stalling;
  • pose as MTX Games staff or another player;
  • try to break, probe or overload the game or its servers.
